Hey there internet neighbour!

My name is Tejaswa Rizyal, but I generally go by *Tej*. There are many reasons for that but the easiest to explain is that I just have always been refered to as Tej since I was a child.

## Pre University

I have been tinkering with computers since I was about 6 years old. Apparently there was a time when I did not understand what things like "shut down" or "folders" meant, but I had just watched my dad using the *magic screen*, and learnt how to use the computer to write poems and play with MSPaint. Credits to my mother for telling me these stories when I was applying for my Bachelors.

At the time we did not have internet access so everything I learnt was purely offline, which I feel was an early sign of me not *enjoying* web development (hence this website). In rural India, with a Tata Photon Plus USB modem in 2012, I had my first interaction with the internet. Which I very much was not allowed to use but ~oh well~.

Later in high school in 2017, I took Computer Science only ever having used HTML (not even CSS) and a month of QBASIC. I'd like to argue that the education relating to CS was not present in my school before that so this was basically the first time I learnt programming.

### Enter C/C++

Yes, I learnt C in school, I still remember the class(es) I had purely for pointers. I think the whole class unanimously agreed that 1 lecture isn't enough and that we need to pend a whole week just learn pointers. I am glad we did that, because C, C++ and pointers have carried on with my throughout my education, so that introduction was the best thing ever.

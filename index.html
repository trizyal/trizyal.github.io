About Me

Hiya! Nice to see you here!

I graduated in 2025 with an MEng BSc in Computer Science with High Performance Graphics and Games Engineering. Today, I've been working professionally in the games industry for over a year at Red Kite Games Limited.

My day-to-day work centers on Unreal Engine 4 & 5, touching renderer code, custom special rendering features, complex frame optimizations, and hardware porting. I have deep hands-on experience with Vulkan, DirectX, and low-level graphics APIs such as NVN2 for Nintendo Switch 2.

Beyond AAA studio production, I am deeply passionate about systems programming, floating-point numeric precision, and building robust developer tooling in Rust and Go. I maintain a heavy Linux workstation workflow utilizing Fedora, Red Hat, and Ubuntu environments.

CPU RAYTRACING // OPTICAL PHYSICS

Raytracer with Optical Physics

A physically-based CPU raytracer designed to simulate complex optical phenomena. Starting from an OpenGL OBJ previewer, I implemented primary ray generation, triangle intersection tests, barycentric interpolation, and CPU-side Blinn-Phong shading.

Advanced features include hard shadow rays with origin displacement to eliminate shadow acne, recursive reflection bounces with energy attenuation, refraction modeled via Snell's Law and Schlick's Fresnel approximation, total internal reflection, and Beer's Law volume absorption for colored transparency.

REAL-TIME PBR // DEFERRED SHADING

Vulkan Renderer

A high-performance real-time rendering application built from scratch in Vulkan. Establishes complete rendering infrastructure including logical device selection, swapchains, framebuffers, and GLFW input handling.

Features Cook-Torrance PBR shading (Beckmann NDF, Schlick Fresnel, Lambertian diffuse), alpha-masking pipelines, custom render-to-texture post-processing pixelation filters with subpass synchronization, and a 4-attachment G-buffer deferred shading pipeline to eliminate over-shading bottlenecks.

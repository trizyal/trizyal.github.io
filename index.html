01 // Background & Profile

About Me

Hiya! Nice to see you here!

I graduated in 2025 with an MEng BSc in Computer Science with High Performance Graphics and Games Engineering. Today, I've been working professionally in the games industry for over a year at [Red Kite Games Limited](http://googleusercontent.com/map_location_reference/1).

My day-to-day work centers on Unreal Engine 4 & 5, touching renderer code, custom special rendering features, complex frame optimizations, and hardware porting. I have deep hands-on experience with Vulkan, DirectX, and low-level graphics APIs such as NVN2 for Nintendo Switch 2.

Outside of AAA console development, I build systems programming tools in Rust and Go, and maintain a Linux workstation running Fedora, Red Hat, and Ubuntu environments.

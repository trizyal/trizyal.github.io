01 // Background & Profile

About Me

Hiya! Nice to see you here!

I graduated in 2025 with an MEng BSc in Computer Science with High Performance Graphics and Games Engineering. Today, I've been working professionally in the games industry for over a year at Red Kite Games.

My day-to-day work centers on Unreal Engine 4 & 5, touching the core renderer code, special rendering features, frame optimizations, and hardware porting. I have deep hands-on experience with Vulkan and DirectX, and I have interacted extensively with NVN2 for the Nintendo Switch 2.

Beyond AAA studio production, I am deeply passionate about systems programming, floating-point numeric precision, and building robust developer tooling in Rust and Go. I maintain a heavy Linux workstation workflow utilizing Fedora, Red Hat, and Ubuntu environments, as well as Windows for platform-specific rendering tasks.
